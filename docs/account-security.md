# Sign-in and security

Turn on two-factor authentication, change your password, and sign other devices out. These settings apply to your account, not to one API key or one cluster.

## Where to find it

1. Open the dashboard.
2. Open **Account settings** from the account menu (the avatar in the top bar).
3. Open **Sign-in & security**.

## Two-factor authentication

1. Turn on **Two-factor authentication**.
2. Scan the code with an authenticator app (1Password, Google Authenticator, Authy, or any app that shows 6-digit codes). If you cannot scan it, click **Copy** and enter the key in the app.
3. Enter the 6-digit code and click **Turn on 2FA**.

The page then shows backup codes. Each code works once at sign-in if you lose the phone. They are shown only then. Click **Copy codes**, store them somewhere safe, then click **I saved them**.

To turn it off, turn the switch off. Enter your password and a code from the app, or a backup code, and click **Turn off 2FA**.

## Password

Click **Change password**. Enter **Current password** and a **New password** of at least **8** characters, then click **Save password**. The new password has to be different from the current one. A wrong current password is rejected.

Saving a new password signs every session out, including this one. Sign in again with the new password.

## Sessions

A dashboard login lasts **24 hours**. After that, sign in again.

**Active sessions** lists devices that are still signed in. **This device** is the browser you are using. **Sign out** ends one other session. **Sign out all other sessions** ends the rest and leaves this device signed in.

**Show all** appears when there are more sessions than the short list.
